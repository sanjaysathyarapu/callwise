# Privacy and security

## What we collect
We collect the information needed to provide service: your name, address, contact details, payment details and the amount of data used. We do not record the websites you visit or the content of your traffic. Support calls are recorded for quality and training and kept for 90 days.

## Sharing your data
We never sell personal data. We share it only with payment processors, installation partners who need your address to visit, and authorities when the law requires it. Partners are bound by contract to use the data only for the task.

## Your rights
You can ask for a copy of your data, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it, under Account, Privacy. We respond within 30 days. Deleting data requires closing the account, and billing records must be kept for seven years for tax reasons even after the account is closed.

## Account security
Turn on two step verification under Account, Security to protect your account. We will never ask for your password by phone, text or email. If you suspect someone accessed your account, change the password and contact support immediately, and we will lock the account while we check recent activity.

## Network protection
Every router includes a firewall that is turned on by default. The optional Safe Browsing feature blocks known malicious sites and is free on all plans; turn it on in the Brightwave app under Security. We do not throttle any traffic type, and we publish our network management practices on the website.
