# Billing and payments

## Billing cycle
Brightwave bills monthly in advance. Your billing date is the day you activated service, and it does not change unless you ask us to move it. The invoice is emailed five days before the billing date and is also available under Account, Invoices. If your activation day is the 29th, 30th or 31st, your billing date moves to the 28th so that every month has one.

## Payment methods
We accept Visa, Mastercard, American Express and direct debit from a checking account. We do not accept cash, cheques or cryptocurrency. Direct debit gives a credit of 5 dollars each month. You can change the payment method at any time under Account, Payment, and the change applies from the next invoice.

## Late payments
If a payment fails, we retry automatically after 3 days and again after 7 days. A late fee of 8 dollars is added when the second retry fails. If the invoice is still unpaid 21 days after the due date, the connection is suspended; it is restored within one hour of payment. Accounts suspended for more than 90 days are closed and the equipment must be returned.

## Disputing a charge
To dispute a charge, contact support within 60 days of the invoice date and tell us the invoice number and the amount in question. While we investigate, the disputed amount is put on hold and you will not be charged a late fee on it. We reply within 5 business days. Approved disputes are refunded to the original payment method within 7 business days.

## Moving house
If you move, tell us at least 10 days before the move. Fiber service can be transferred to the new address if it is inside our coverage area, and the transfer fee is 49 dollars. If the new address is not covered, you can cancel without paying an early termination fee. Your final bill is prorated to the day of disconnection.
