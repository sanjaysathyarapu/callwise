# Plans and pricing

## Home 300
Home 300 gives symmetrical speeds of 300 megabits per second and costs 45 dollars a month. It suits households with up to five devices streaming or working at the same time. It includes one router and has no data cap.

## Home 1000
Home 1000 gives symmetrical speeds of 1 gigabit per second and costs 70 dollars a month. It includes a Wi-Fi 6 router and one mesh extender. This is the plan we recommend for homes larger than 2,500 square feet or with more than ten connected devices.

## Business 500
Business 500 costs 90 dollars a month and gives 500 megabits per second with a static IP address and priority support. Business plans require a 12 month agreement. Priority support calls are answered within two minutes during support hours.

## Contracts and cancellation
Home plans have no contract and can be cancelled at any time with no fee. Cancel under Account, Plan, or by calling support. Service ends at the end of the current billing period and we do not prorate the final month. Business plans cancelled before the 12 months are complete are charged 50 percent of the remaining months.

## Equipment
Routers and extenders remain our property and must be returned within 30 days of cancellation. We send a prepaid return box. Equipment that is not returned is charged at 120 dollars for a router and 60 dollars for an extender. You may use your own router instead and we will take 5 dollars a month off your bill.

## Promotions
New customers get the first month free on any home plan when they sign up online. The offer cannot be combined with other discounts and applies once per address. Customers who refer a friend receive a 25 dollar credit after the second payment of the friend they referred.
