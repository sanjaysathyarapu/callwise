# Support and installation

## Contact and hours
Phone support is open Monday to Friday from 7 AM to 9 PM and on weekends from 9 AM to 5 PM, Eastern time. The number is 1-800-555-0177. Live chat in the app is available during the same hours and usually answers within three minutes. Email at help@brightwave.example is answered within one business day.

## Installation appointments
After you order, we offer installation windows of four hours, in the morning or afternoon. A technician must be able to reach the wall where the terminal will be mounted, and an adult over 18 must be present. Installation is free for home plans. Rescheduling is free if you tell us at least 24 hours before the window; later changes cost 25 dollars.

## What the technician does
The technician runs the fiber line from the street to your home, mounts the optical terminal on an interior wall, installs the router and tests the connection on a wired and a wireless device. A standard installation takes two to three hours. If the home needs a new conduit or an underground bore, we survey first and the work can take a further week.

## Coverage
We offer service in areas where fiber has been laid to your street. Enter your address on our website to check coverage. If your address is not covered you can join the waiting list, and we email you when construction reaches your street. Apartment buildings need the owner's permission before we can install.

## Accessibility
Support is available by text relay and video relay for customers who are deaf or hard of hearing. Large print and braille invoices can be requested at no cost. Tell the support agent at the start of the call and they will arrange it.
