# Troubleshooting

## No internet connection
First check the light on the optical terminal on your wall. A solid green light means the fiber line is working. A blinking red light means the line is cut or damaged and a technician is needed; do not try to repair the fiber cable yourself. If the light is green, restart the router by unplugging it for 30 seconds and plugging it back in. Wait three minutes for it to start fully before testing again.

## Slow speeds
Run a speed test with a computer connected to the router by cable, because Wi-Fi speed depends on distance and walls. If the wired result is within 10 percent of your plan speed, the line is fine and the slowdown is in the Wi-Fi. Move closer to the router, switch to the 5 gigahertz network, or add a mesh extender. If the wired result is far below your plan speed, contact support and give us the result.

## Wi-Fi password and network name
The default network name and password are printed on a label under the router. You can change them in the Brightwave app under Wi-Fi settings. After you change them every device must reconnect with the new password. If you forget the new password, hold the reset button on the router for 10 seconds to restore the label values; this erases all custom settings.

## Outages
Check status.brightwave.example for current outages in your area. Large outages are posted within 15 minutes. If your area is not listed, report it in the app under Help, Report an outage. For outages that last longer than 24 hours we credit one thirtieth of the monthly price for each full day without service, and the credit is applied automatically.

## Router lights
A solid white light on the router means everything is working. A pulsing white light means the router is starting up. An amber light means the router cannot reach our network; check the cable between the router and the wall terminal. A red light means a hardware fault, and the router should be replaced free of charge.
